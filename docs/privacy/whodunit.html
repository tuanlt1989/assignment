This privacy policy applies to the Android app Who Did It? Logic Clue Puzzle, package com.tuanapps.whodunit, published on Google Play by the developer MQ Co.,Ltd (“we”, “us”).

What the app stores

Your puzzle progress, times, streak and settings are stored only on your device. The app has no user accounts and we do not run servers that receive this data. Uninstalling the app or using “Reset progress” deletes it.

Advertising and tracking

The app shows no ads, does not use the advertising ID and contains no analytics or tracking. We do not collect personal data.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; Google Play tells the app only whether the purchase is active.

Children

The game is a general-audience puzzle game. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
