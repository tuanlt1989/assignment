Privacy Policy

What the app stores

Your answers, saved questions, exam history and settings are stored only on your device. The app has no user accounts and we do not run servers that receive this data. Uninstalling the app or using “Reset progress” deletes it.

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, app interactions and diagnostic data. In regions where consent is required (for example the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent using Google’s consent form, and you can change your choice anytime under Settings → Privacy & ad choices. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; Google Play tells the app only whether the purchase is active.

Children

The app is a study tool for adults preparing for a professional license exam and is not directed to children under 13.
